01 What is RTT?

A table where
ideas get moving.

RTT Jogja is a community program connecting academia and industry in an informal setting.

We gather future leaders, practitioners, students, and builders to share perspectives on entrepreneurship, innovation, technology, hospitality, education, and personal growth.

Dialogue becomes development. Connections become opportunities. The work below is the public record of what we have done.
